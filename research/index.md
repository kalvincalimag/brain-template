# Research Index

Source material, separated from durable memory. Each entry records its question, date, source links or provenance, freshness, findings, uncertainties, and any follow-up needed.

Recommended layout:

```text
research/
├── memos/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md
├── clips/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>/
└── index.md
```

Research findings do not become project truth automatically. Promote only reviewed, stable conclusions into `memory/`.
