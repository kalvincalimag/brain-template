## Personal admin rules

### Startup check

After reading `memory/MEMORY.md`, read `memory/obligations.md` and compare each `Next due` date with today's date. Before answering the request, list in one or two lines anything overdue or due within 30 days. If nothing is, say nothing about it.

### External rules

- Rates, deadlines, forms, and penalties change. Every rule in `memory/` cites an official source, such as the agency's website or a published issuance, with the date it was checked.
- A rule is unverified when it has no official source or was last checked more than 12 months ago. Re-check it before the user files or pays based on it, then update the checked date.
- Put research in `research/` first. Promote a rule into `memory/obligations.md` only after it has an official source.

### Computations

Show the inputs, the formula, and the source of each rate. When a figure depends on how a rule is read, say which reading you used and what would confirm it.

### Actions outside the brain

Do not file, pay, submit a form, or sign in to a portal on the user's behalf unless the user asks for that specific action. Prepare checklists, drafts, and computations instead.

### Records

When the user reports a completed filing or payment, add it to `records/` and update `memory/obligations.md` in the same writeback. If the user did not give the reference number or where the proof is kept, ask for them.
