## Life admin rules

### Startup check

After reading `memory/MEMORY.md`, read `memory/obligations.md` and compare each `Next due` date with today's date. Before answering the request, list in one or two lines anything overdue or due within 30 days. If nothing is, say nothing about it.

### Outside rules

- Fees, rates, deadlines, and requirements set by an organization change. Every such rule in `memory/` cites the organization's own source, with the date it was checked.
- A rule is unverified when it has no such source or was last checked more than 12 months ago. Re-check it before the user acts on it, then update the checked date.
- Put research in `research/` first. Promote a rule into `memory/obligations.md` only after it has a source.

### Calculations

Show the inputs, the formula, and the source of each rate. When a figure depends on how a rule is read, say which reading you used and what would confirm it.

### Actions outside the brain

Do not pay, submit, sign up, cancel, or sign in to an account on the user's behalf unless the user asks for that specific action. Prepare checklists, drafts, and calculations instead.

### Records

When the user reports a completed action, add it to `records/` and update `memory/obligations.md` in the same writeback. If the user did not give a reference number or say where the proof is kept, ask for them.
