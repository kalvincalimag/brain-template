# Procedures

Step-by-step ways to get recurring tasks done, such as making a payment or renewing a document. Write one section per procedure, from what actually worked. Link it from the obligation's "How to do it" cell in `memory/obligations.md`.

Each procedure records:

- what it is for and which obligation it serves;
- the steps, in order, with the channel used;
- what to have ready before starting;
- what proof to keep afterward;
- the date the steps were last confirmed to work, and the source if an organization published them.

A procedure not confirmed in the last 12 months is unverified. Check it before relying on it.
