# Profile: life-admin

## Use when

The brain helps a person keep track of recurring personal responsibilities: anything with a due date, accounts held with organizations, documents to keep, and a record of what was done. It works as a personal secretary that remembers between sessions.

## Do not use when

The purpose is a software or work project. Use `software-project` for that.

## What it adds

- `memory/profile.md`, facts about the person that decide which responsibilities apply.
- `memory/obligations.md`, every recurring responsibility with its due rule, next due date, and source.
- `memory/documents.md`, where each important document is kept.
- `memory/procedures.md`, step-by-step ways to get recurring tasks done.
- `records/`, a log of completed actions by year and organization.
- Rules in `AGENTS.md` for a due-date check at startup, sourcing outside rules, showing calculations, and never acting outside the brain without a request.

## Interview

Ask these when creating the brain. Record only what the user states, dated, in the files named. Mark anything the user is unsure of as unverified.

1. Which areas should this brain look after? (`memory/overview.md` under "In scope")
2. Do any rules depend on where the person lives? If so, where? (`memory/profile.md`)
3. Which organizations and accounts are involved? Ask for the last four digits of any account or ID number only. (`memory/profile.md`)
4. What is due soon, or already behind? (`memory/obligations.md`, unverified until sourced)
5. Where are documents and receipts kept? (`memory/documents.md`)

## Boundaries

This profile holds structure only. The areas a brain covers, and any rules, rates, or deadlines, belong in that brain, with sources, never in the template.

## Known gaps

- The due-date check runs only when a session starts in the brain. Nothing reminds the person between sessions.
